How It Works

01

AI Phrase Dictionary

Scans for 300+ phrases that AI models overuse — "ensure that", "leverage", "in order to" — and replaces each with natural human equivalents.

02

Burstiness Injection

The #1 signal Turnitin checks. AI writes at uniform sentence length. We split long sentences and occasionally merge short ones to create human-like rhythm variation.

03

Discourse Markers

Inserts natural conversation anchors — "That said,", "In practice,", "Worth noting here —" — to break the mechanical paragraph-to-paragraph progression AI produces.

04

Voice & Contraction

Converts passive constructions to active voice and injects contractions where they feel natural — both strong signals of genuine human authorship.
